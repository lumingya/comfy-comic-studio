# Mobile access

Connect your phone and the Mio computer to the same reachable network. Open the computer's Mio address in your phone browser.

The bottom navigation scrolls horizontally. Forms use a single-column layout, and the reader supports continuous scrolling.

If connection fails, check the computer's IP address, port, firewall and service. Use the computer's LAN address on your phone.
